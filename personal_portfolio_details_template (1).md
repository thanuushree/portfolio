# Personal Portfolio — Details Collection Template

Use this document to collect the information needed to personalize your portfolio website. Replace the square-bracket placeholders with your own details. If a field is optional or you do not know the answer yet, leave it blank or write `Skip`.

> **Privacy note:** Only publish details you are comfortable making public. A phone number, personal email, exact location, grades, and resume may reveal private information. You can use a professional email or a contact form instead of displaying a phone number.

## 6. Website Preferences (Already Selected)

These preferences are based on the current portfolio plan.

- **Framework:** React
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Animation:** Framer Motion
- **Theme:** Dark background with neon/glow accents
- **Motion style:** Smooth scrolling, hover effects, and page transitions
- **Layout:** Responsive, mobile-first design
- **Suggested sections:** Hero, About, Skills, Projects, Experience, Education, Contact, Resume, and Footer
- **Optional sections:** Testimonials and Blog

### Suggested visual direction

- Near-black or deep navy background
- High-contrast text
- Cyan or violet accents used selectively
- Clean typography and consistent spacing
- Subtle card borders and glows
- Brief, purposeful animations
- Keyboard-accessible controls and reduced-motion support
